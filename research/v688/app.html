How a question is chosen

Rank is salience × gain × urgency, as a product so a zero anywhere is fatal. Salience is activation in the buffer, decaying each cycle. Gain is bits of the candidate set a question removes, computed on the same predicate trie the reasoner stores with. Urgency comes from what kind of hole is being filled.
